DiffusionNexus does not distribute FFmpeg.

When no ffmpeg/ffprobe pair is present on the machine, the application downloads a
prebuilt FFmpeg from ffbinaries.com - a third-party mirror, not the FFmpeg project's
own distribution - and executes it as a separate process. Those binaries are covered
by their own license, which is distributed with them, and are expected to be GPL:
the Windows builds served by ffbinaries.com are configured with GPL-licensed
components such as libx264.

later. See https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
